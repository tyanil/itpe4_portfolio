About Me

Hello! I'm Chanelle, an aspiring Program Manager with a passion for understanding users and creating meaningful digital experiences. I believe that good design starts with empathy by listening, observing, and learning from real people. Though I'm still developing my skills, I'm dedicated to improving each day and using what I learn to design solutions that are thoughtful, inclusive, and useful.

My journey in UX began with curiosity, wanting to know how designs shape the way people interact with technology. Along the way, I discovered how much I enjoy combining creativity with strategy, and how collaboration brings ideas to life. Whether I'm working on a Figma project, coding a webpage, or organizing tasks, I always aim to make every step purposeful and user-centered.

I may still be learning, but I'm excited about where this path will take me. Every project, challenge, and feedback helps me grow, not just as a designer, but as a future program manager who values people, process, and progress.
